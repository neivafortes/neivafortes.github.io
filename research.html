Job market paper

The Effects of State-Mandated Elementary School Recess Laws on Discriminatory Bullying

Presented at FLX Economics of Education Workshop (2026); scheduled at APPAM (poster) and SEA (2026)

Using nationwide school-level civil-rights data and staggered-adoption, synthetic-control, and synthetic difference-in-differences designs, this paper examines how state recess mandates affect discriminatory bullying allegations. Results indicate increases in reported sex-based allegations following strong mandates, while estimates for race- and disability-based allegations are less consistent. The findings are consistent with changes in exposure, detection, or reporting during unstructured school time.

NBER Working Paper No. 35293

The Effect of Height on Adolescents’ Body Image Perceptions and Behaviors

With Monica Deza and Maria Zhu · June 2026

This paper estimates the causal effect of height on adolescents’ body image, encompassing self-perceptions of weight, the accuracy of those perceptions, and weight-management aspirations. Using the NLSY97, we leverage within-individual variation in height during adolescence and condition on BMI. Taller adolescents are more likely to perceive themselves as overweight, with perception and behavioral responses differing by gender.
